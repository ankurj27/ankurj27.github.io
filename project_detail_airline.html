Airline Customer Satisfaction Analysis Report

Understanding Passenger Experiences and Identifying Improvement Opportunities

❮ ❯

Overview

This project focuses on analyzing airline passenger satisfaction using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw survey data into meaningful insights that help understand how operational factors (like delays) and service quality influence overall customer experience. The dashboard is designed with a business-first approach, enabling quick decision-making through high-level KPIs and deeper service-level analysis.

Key Insights

  • Arrival delays have a strong negative impact on customer satisfaction.
  • Business class passengers consistently report higher satisfaction compared to economy.
  • Digital services such as WiFi and online booking show lower ratings and improvement scope.
  • Core services like cleanliness, seat comfort, and onboard service are key drivers of satisfaction.
  • Customer experience varies significantly across travel type and service categories.

The Takeaway

    This project highlights the importance of combining operational metrics with customer experience data to get a complete picture.
  • It also demonstrates how proper data transformation (like unpivoting) and structured visualization can significantly improve the quality of insights.
  • Most importantly, it reinforces that effective dashboards are not just about visuals — they are about answering real business questions.

Tools Used
